1. Overview & Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how SafeNest ("SafeNest", "we", "our", or "us") collects, processes, stores, uses, and protects information when parents and legal guardians use the SafeNest Android application and related cloud synchronization services.

SafeNest is engineered around an on-device-first privacy architecture: DNS domain filtering, category time-limit enforcement, notification safety analysis, screen-time rhythm detection, weekly wellbeing summaries, and baseline AI safety coaching execute locally on your Android device whenever possible.

Core Privacy Commitments: SafeNest does not sell or rent personal data, does not display third-party advertising, does not route general internet traffic through remote VPN servers, does not upload raw message contents or full web browsing histories to SafeNest servers, and never uploads or stores your personal BYOK Gemini API key in the cloud.

2. Information We Collect & Process

Depending on which features and optional Android permissions you enable, SafeNest processes data in two layers: (a) strictly on-device data that never leaves your phone, and (b) authenticated cloud account data stored in your Google Firebase project partition to synchronize parental rules across sessions.

2.2 Data Processed Strictly On-Device (Never Uploaded to SafeNest Cloud Servers)

  • Local DNS Queries (Android VpnService): When the Local DNS Shield is active, SafeNest intercepts only UDP port 53 DNS lookup packets on a local loopback interface (10.0.0.1). Blocked domains (including custom blocklists and Category Time Limit blocks) are sinkholed locally to 0.0.0.0 / ::. Only allowed domain lookups are forwarded to Cloudflare Family DNS (1.1.1.3 / 1.0.0.3). Non-DNS packets bypass the VPN interface untouched, and full browsing histories are never uploaded to SafeNest servers.
  • Raw Notification Text (Transient Evaluation): If you grant Notification Access, notifications are evaluated in volatile memory by SafeNest's on-device engine and immediately discarded. Only redacted risk indicators are retained if an alert is triggered.
  • App Usage & Weekly Trends (PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS): Daily app-category usage and 7-day comparisons are calculated on-device to enforce category budgets and show weekly wellbeing trends.
  • Parent PIN & Child Guard State: Your 4-digit Parent PIN is salted and hashed locally using SHA-256 in Android Jetpack DataStore. Plaintext PINs are never stored or transmitted.
  • Personal BYOK Gemini API Key: Stored exclusively in local Jetpack DataStore (see Section 4).
  • Local Prompt & Interaction Counters: Lightweight counters (session counts, dismissal timestamps) stored locally in Jetpack DataStore solely to respect cooldowns for optional update, feedback, or share prompts.

2.3 Information SafeNest Does Not Collect

SafeNest does not collect precise or background GPS location, contacts, SMS/MMS messages, phone call logs, microphone audio, continuous camera feeds, financial/payment card numbers, or advertising identifiers (AAID) for ad targeting.

3. How We Use Data & Legal Bases

We process information strictly for the following purposes:

  • Providing Parental Control & Core Safety Features: Authenticating the parent account, applying DNS filtering and category time limits, enforcing Parent PIN lockouts, and syncing family rules across parent sessions.
  • Generating Digital Wellbeing Insights & Alerts: Calculating daily screen-time budgets, flagging late-night anomalies, delivering weekly coaching summaries, and surfacing privacy-redacted safety alerts.
  • Powering Optional AI Safety Coaching: Answering parent questions, evaluating suspicious apps/links/screenshots requested by the parent, and enforcing user-configured daily AI quota caps and automatic fallbacks.
  • Service Integrity & Security: Enforcing per-user Firestore access rules and protecting against unauthorized account access or quota abuse.

Legal Bases (EEA, UK, and Swiss Users): Depending on the processing activity, we rely on: (1) Performance of a contract (providing requested parental control and sync features); (2) Consent (when you explicitly grant optional system permissions such as VPN, Usage Access, Notification Access, Notifications, or choose to use optional BYOK Cloud AI and Photo Picker); and (3) Legitimate interests (maintaining app reliability, security, and child digital safety without commercial profiling).

4. AI-Based Safety Features & BYOK Policy

SafeNest integrates two complementary AI safety systems designed to minimize cloud exposure and keep parents in control:

4.1 On-Device Notification Safety Engine & Tier 3 AI Coach (Zero Cloud Transmission)

  • 3-Stage Local NLP Pipeline: Screens incoming notification titles/snippets using local lexicon matching, phonetic/leetspeak obfuscation normalization, and semantic scoring for cyberbullying, self-harm risk, predatory grooming, and scams/phishing. Processing finishes locally on-device with zero external API calls.
  • Tier 3 On-Device Free Coach & Risk Advisor: Provides offline parenting scripts, domain/app risk checks, and weekly coaching briefs directly on the device at zero cost and with zero network transmission.

4.2 Optional BYOK Cloud AI Coaching (Bring Your Own Key)

Parents may optionally paste their own personal Google Gemini API key in AI Engine & BYOK Plan settings to enable deeper cloud responses (Tier 1 Standard or Tier 2 Eco Lite) and visual screenshot risk analysis.

  • Strictly Optional & Local Key Storage: Your API key is saved only inside on-device Jetpack DataStore and transmitted directly from your device to Google's Gemini REST endpoint (generativelanguage.googleapis.com) in the x-goog-api-key header. SafeNest servers and Firebase Firestore never receive, log, or store your key.
  • Automatic Local PII Redaction: Before any text prompt leaves the device in BYOK mode, SafeNest runs local redaction to replace email addresses ([EMAIL]), phone numbers ([NUMBER]), 4–8 digit OTP/verification codes ([CODE]), and social handles ([USER]).
  • Context Minimization & Deduplication: Cloud prompts include only summarized child context (age, daily limit, general alert count, aggregated weekly minutes — never raw child identities or full message histories), use a capped 6-turn sliding window, and reuse a 45-second local cache to prevent duplicate requests.
  • Smart Cascade & Budget Guardrails: If you enable Eco Lite Mode, reach 80% of your daily budget cap, or hit a provider rate limit (HTTP 429), SafeNest automatically cascades to Eco Lite or falls back seamlessly to Tier 3 On-Device Free mode.
  • Human-in-the-Loop Decision Support: AI outputs provide advisory parenting suggestions and risk assessments only. SafeNest never takes autonomous legal, medical, or disciplinary actions without guardian review.

4.3 Zero-Permission Android Photo Picker (PickVisualMedia)

When checking a suspicious screenshot in the Risk Advisor, SafeNest invokes the system Android Photo Picker so the app requires no broad storage or gallery permissions. Only the single image explicitly selected by the parent is decoded and downscaled in memory (maximum 1024px JPEG), is never persisted to disk or SafeNest cloud storage, and is transmitted only to the parent's configured BYOK Gemini API endpoint when BYOK mode is active and the parent taps Analyze Safety Risk.

5. Data Sharing, Disclosure & No-Sale Policy

No Sale or Advertising Sharing: SafeNest does not sell personal data (including children's data) for monetary or other valuable consideration under CCPA/CPRA or any privacy law, and does not share data with third-party ad networks, analytics brokers, or data marketplaces.

Data is shared or transmitted only in the following strictly limited circumstances:

  • Cloud Infrastructure Subprocessors: Google Firebase (Authentication & Cloud Firestore) processes account identity, parent-configured rules, redacted alert summaries, and numeric AI quota counters under Google's enterprise data protection terms.
  • DNS Resolution Provider: When Local DNS Shield is enabled, non-blocked domain hostnames (UDP port 53 DNS queries only) are sent to Cloudflare Family DNS (1.1.1.3 / 1.0.0.3) to resolve IP addresses and enforce network-level malware/adult filtering.
  • User-Configured BYOK AI Provider: Only when a parent explicitly enables BYOK Cloud mode and supplies their own Gemini API key are redacted prompts (and optional single selected screenshots) sent directly to Google's Gemini API under the parent's own API key terms.
  • Legal Obligations & Safety: We may disclose information only if required to comply with valid legal process (such as a court order or subpoena) or to protect against imminent physical harm.
  • Share Sheet & Store Links: Using the optional "Share SafeNest" action opens the Android system share sheet with SafeNest's Play Store link only—never family rules, alerts, or personal data.

8. Android Permissions & Prominent Disclosures

SafeNest follows Google Play's principle of least privilege. Before any sensitive permission or special system access is opened, SafeNest presents a clear in-app prominent disclosure explaining why the access is requested and lets you decline without blocking unrelated features.

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required for parent Google Sign-In, Firestore synchronization, forwarding allowed DNS queries, and optional parent-initiated BYOK Gemini requests.
  • BIND_VPN_SERVICE (Local DNS Shield): Optional; creates a local loopback VPN interface exclusively to filter UDP port 53 DNS lookups on-device. Does not tunnel non-DNS traffic or route through remote SafeNest VPN servers.
  • PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS (Usage Access): Optional; granted in Android Settings only if the parent enables device screen-time sync, Category Time Limits, and Weekly Wellbeing Reports.
  • BIND_NOTIFICATION_LISTENER_SERVICE (Notification Access): Optional; granted in Android Settings only if the parent enables on-device 3-stage safety alert scanning.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE & FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USE: Keeps the Local DNS Shield running reliably with a persistent status notification while enabled by the user.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Optional (Android 13+); used for the Local DNS Shield status indicator, on-device safety alert notifications, and optional Weekly Screen-Time Summary notifications.
  • REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONS: Optional; offered in Device Health settings so OEM battery savers do not silently terminate the Local DNS Shield.
  • Launcher Visibility (<queries> for ACTION_MAIN / CATEGORY_LAUNCHER): Allows SafeNest to map launchable installed apps into screen-time categories (Games, Video, Social, etc.) without requesting the broad QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES permission.

SafeNest does not declare or request READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, READ_MEDIA_IMAGES, READ_MEDIA_VIDEO, CAMERA, RECORD_AUDIO, ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION, READ_CONTACTS, or READ_SMS.

9. Data Security, Retention & International Transfers

  • Encryption & Access Controls: All network communication with Firebase, Cloudflare, and optional Gemini API uses TLS encryption in transit. Cloud Firestore encrypts data at rest and enforces zero-trust security rules restricting every family document, alert, summary, and usageQuota ledger strictly to request.auth.uid == userId.
  • On-Device Cryptographic Protection: The optional Parent PIN is hashed locally using SHA-256 with a domain salt. Personal BYOK keys remain sandboxed inside private app DataStore storage.
  • Retention Schedule: Cloud family profiles, rules, redacted alerts, and quota counters remain stored while your parent account is active, until you delete individual records in-app or request account deletion. Local DataStore records are erased immediately if you clear app storage or uninstall SafeNest.
  • International Data Transfers: Google Firebase and Cloudflare operate globally distributed infrastructure. Where data crosses borders (such as from the EEA/UK to the United States), transfers rely on recognized safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) or applicable adequacy frameworks maintained by those providers.

10. Your Privacy Rights & Controls

Regardless of where you live—and specifically under the EU/UK GDPR, California CCPA/CPRA, and comparable privacy laws—you have the right to:

  • Access & Portability: View your stored child profiles, schedules, blocklists, usage summaries, redacted alerts, and AI quota counters directly inside SafeNest or request a copy via email.
  • Correction & Update: Edit child profile nicknames, age bands, limits, schedules, and custom blocklists anytime in the app.
  • Deletion (Right to Be Forgotten): Delete child profiles, alerts, or custom blocklists in-app; clear your local BYOK API key with one tap; uninstall SafeNest to wipe all on-device storage; or request complete deletion of your Firebase Authentication account and all Firestore records by emailing tuyenctbk@gmail.com from your registered Google email with subject "SafeNest Account Deletion Request" (fulfilled within 30 days).
  • Withdraw Consent & Revoke Permissions: Disable Local DNS Shield, Usage Access, Notification Access, Post Notifications, or BYOK Cloud AI at any time in SafeNest or Android System Settings without affecting your ability to use SafeNest's core non-dependent features.
  • Objection, Restriction & Non-Discrimination: Object to or restrict processing, exercise CCPA/CPRA opt-out/limit rights (SafeNest neither sells nor shares personal data for cross-context behavioral advertising), and receive equal service without discrimination.
  • Supervisory Authority Complaint: EEA, UK, or Swiss residents may lodge a complaint with their local data protection authority if they believe processing violates applicable law.
